    You stand in the courtyard of the dead and feel, suddenly and absolutely, that you are not meant to be here. The servants go past. The warm air does not move. You turn and walk back out the way you came, without running, because running might be noticed.

    The fen is cold. The abbey is quiet. By the time you reach Marrowmere, the water in the town's ditches has risen an inch, and the reeve does not ask what happened. He only looks at you for a long moment and writes your name in the ledger beside the others.

    The flood does not stop. But you live.
